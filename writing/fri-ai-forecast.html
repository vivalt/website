Research March 31, 2026

FRI's survey of how AI will affect the U.S. economy has dropped. How does it line up with reality?

The survey was conducted October 2025 - February 2026. Baseline values provided in the survey have already been updated in public data in some cases. But, they will continue to update over time as more data become available.

I asked an AI agent to generate some plots that can be easily updated as these data come out. Here is what it said (disclaimer: lightly vetted, some mistakes may remain):
