A Note on Pre-Analysis Plans in Economics

I've developed EarlyReview, a tool that helps researchers evaluate their pre-analysis plans and registered reports for completeness, clarity, and consistency before official registration.

Key Findings

I tested the tool on publicly available plans from the AEA RCT Registry and found performance varied significantly across different dimensions:

Excellent Performance (100%): All plans included foundational information such as research questions, study design, randomization units, treatment arms, and analysis units.

Good Performance (85-95%): Most plans described target populations, assignment mechanisms, estimate types (ITT), sample sizes, covariates, and regression equations—though not universally.

Problematic Areas (55-70%): Plans frequently lacked clear strategies for addressing attrition or clustering standard errors. Primary outcomes and main specifications often contained inconsistencies or lacked clarity.

Significant Gaps (<50%): Multiple hypothesis testing procedures remained unspecified or unclear. Power calculations and Minimum Detectable Effects (MDEs) were often missing or unrealistic.

Notable Statistics

The average pre-analysis plan passed approximately 80% of checks. Very few plans tested passed all the checks. Journal of Development Economics Registered Reports performed substantially better, as expected given their greater detail requirements.

Purpose and Access

The tool provides detailed feedback through comments while nudging researchers toward improved practices. Users can upload plans confidentially at no cost and access free screening at EarlyReview.ai.
